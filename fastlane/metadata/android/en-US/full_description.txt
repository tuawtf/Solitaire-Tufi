Solitaire is a modern, ad-free, tracker-free, and 100% offline Klondike Solitaire card game.

Features:
- Material 3 Design: Dynamic color support, light/dark themes, and modern UI.
- Customizable Controls: Tap-to-place or traditional drag-and-drop.
- Classic Klondike Rules: Draw 1 or Draw 3 modes.
- Multiple Difficulties: Easy, Regular, and Hard deals.
- Sound & Haptics: Synthesized card audio effects and customizable haptic feedback.
- Private & Offline: Requires no internet permission and tracks zero personal data.
